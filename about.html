Scripture first.
Your studies, in your hands.

Open Study is a free Bible reading and study workspace. Read, search, reflect, and download your work. No account, subscription, or AI service is required.

Files you can keep

Save study downloads a new JSON snapshot containing your title, timestamps, WEB translation identifier, scripture references, and study content. Open study restores that snapshot on another device or browser. Later changes need another save. The app cannot silently update your original file.

The browser may keep a recovery copy on this device. Clearing browser data, private browsing, or storage limits can remove it. Downloaded snapshots are your durable backups. Files contain your notes in plain text: share them thoughtfully and do not enter passwords or API keys.

Privacy

Your notes and imported files are processed in your browser and are not uploaded. There are no analytics, third-party fonts, advertising scripts, or AI requests. GitHub Pages serves the app and Bible assets and may log ordinary website requests under GitHub’s privacy policy. Clear browser recovery in the notebook on a shared device.

World English Bible

This release includes the 66 Old and New Testament books from the original application’s World English Bible dataset. Verse wording is preserved; paragraph and poetry segments are joined for display. Footnotes and original layout markers are not included.

The World English Bible is public domain and may be freely redistributed. Its name is a trademark used here to identify the translation. See the publisher’s rights statement and World English Bible website. No endorsement is implied.

A thoughtful starting point

The Observe, Understand, and Respond prompts are study aids, not generated commentary. Search matches the words you type; it does not infer themes or answer questions. Compare the surrounding chapters and distinguish scripture from your own interpretation.

A separate new beginning

This static edition is separate from the earlier Biblical Bot cloud application. Cloud studies do not appear automatically here. Keep your original exports; the repository includes migration guidance. AI, other translations, Markdown export, and cloud synchronization are not part of this release.
